1. The idea

In Experiment 1 we fed a constant current and got a steady rhythm. A real neuron never gets a constant input: it gets signals that rise and fall over time. This experiment asks how the spike pattern encodes a changing input.

We keep the same LIF neuron (tau, threshold, reset) but replace the constant current with a slowly-changing one, for example a sine or a ramp.
