1. The idea

The two-spiral dataset is a classic: two interleaved arms, each class curls around the other. It is the standard example of a boundary that is hard but learnable - needs a curved decision boundary and enough capacity.

  • Plain ANN - our reference, trained normally. Should learn it.
  • Spiking net - same task with LIF neurons and a smooth activation. We compare several activations.

We compare both against the correct answer, and track accuracy, energy, and speed - as in Experiment 3.
