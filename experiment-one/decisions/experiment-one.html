1. The idea, in one breath

A normal network passes numbers around. A spiking network passes events (spikes) that happen at moments in time. Each neuron has a little internal "voltage." Input nudges it up; when it passes a threshold, the neuron fires a spike and resets. The timing of the spikes carries the information.

2. The LIF neuron, in one picture

LIF = Leaky Integrate-and-Fire. "Leaky" because the voltage keeps draining a little, like water in a leaky bucket.

  • Integrate: input raises the voltage (the rising curve).
  • Fire: when it touches the threshold line, it emits a spike.
  • Reset: right after, the voltage drops to a low value.
  • Leak: between inputs, the voltage gently drains back down.
