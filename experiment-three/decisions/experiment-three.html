1. The idea

We train two networks on the same simple task, XOR (output 1 if the two inputs differ), then compare them against the correct answer.

  • Plain ANN - a small standard network, trained with normal backprop. Our reference.
  • Spiking net - the same task, but with LIF neurons and surrogate gradient training. We try 4 different surrogate functions.

XOR is the classic "needs a hidden layer" task, so if the spiking net learns it, the whole training pipeline works.
